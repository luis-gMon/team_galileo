Topic Proposal: Data-Driven Personalized Workout Optimization System

Our research focuses on developing a comprehensive workout recommendation system that optimizes exercise routines based on individual user profiles, physiological responses, and workout efficiency patterns. We aim to create personalized fitness plans that maximize both physical benefits and mood improvements through data-driven analysis. Exercise plays a crucial role in maintaining overall health, especially in today's world where obesity rates continue to rise. These plans aim to help the public adopt sustainable fitness routines that improve both physical and mental well-being.

SMART Questions:

    - What combination of workout types and durations maximizes calorie burn efficiency for different user profiles (clustered by age, BMI, and fitness level)?
    - How does the relationship between heart rate patterns and calorie burn vary across different workout types, and can this predict optimal workout intensity?
    - Can we identify distinct user profiles based on their workout patterns using clustering analysis on workout duration, calorie burn, and heart rate?
    - Is the average heart rate across different workout types (Cycling, Cardio, Strength, Yoga) or BMI levels the same?
    - Is there a link between poor sleep (fewer hours and higher resting heart rate) and increased feelings of fatigue or stress after workouts?


Data Source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/adilshamim8/workout-and-fitness-tracker-data
Our analysis will utilize a comprehensive fitness dataset containing 10,000+ workout sessions from diverse users. The dataset includes key variables such as User ID, demographic information (age, gender, height, weight), workout metrics (type, duration, calories burned), and physiological measurements (heart rate, steps taken).
